How I design

The same habits show up in every sample above.

Start from the exam

Objectives and practice questions mirror what CAPS assessments ask, including how marks are awarded.

Show, then do

Numbered steps with screenshots, then a learner task with a visible result to check against.

Feedback in every item

Questions explain why an answer is right, so assessment keeps teaching.

A theme with a purpose

The retro desktop look mirrors the software being taught and keeps long lessons easy to scan.
